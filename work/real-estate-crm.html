Real estate CRM

A CRM for a real estate team to track leads, site visits and deals in one place, with follow-ups handled automatically.

Real estateCRMAutomation
IndustryReal estate
ServicesCRM build, automation
TypeInternal tool
The problem

What needed solving

Leads were arriving from several places, and the team tracked them across chats and spreadsheets. It was hard to see who had been contacted, which site visits were booked, and which deals were close to closing.

What I built

The solution

  • A single pipeline for every lead, from first enquiry to closed deal
  • Site visit scheduling attached to each lead
  • Deal tracking so the team can see what is close to closing
  • Automatic follow-ups so no new lead waits for a reply
Outcome

Where it landed

The team works from one shared view of every lead, and routine follow-ups happen without anyone having to remember them.
