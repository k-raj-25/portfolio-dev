Lawyer directory platform

A law firm's website with a directory of verified lawyers, an approval workflow for new profiles, and privacy built in so clients always go through the firm.

The brief

What needed solving

A law firm wanted a public directory of verified lawyers that clients can search, while making sure every client conversation goes through the firm and never straight to a lawyer's phone.

What I built

The solution

  • Search by state, city, practice area, experience, language, court, fee and online consultation
  • A five-step lawyer registration with Bar enrolment proof and ID upload
  • An approval workflow: profiles stay pending until the firm approves them, rejects them with a reason, or later suspends them, with an email at every step
  • Contact only through the firm: a helpline, a WhatsApp message pre-filled with the lawyer's profile ID, or a callback request with a reference number
  • Every profile edit saved as a before-and-after change for the firm to review
  • A blog with editor accounts that can manage only their own posts
Under the hood

How it's built

  • Lawyers' private details and documents never reach public pages; documents live on private storage and every view is logged
  • Rate limits, honeypots and time-traps on forms to stop spam and scraping
  • Legal pages written for Indian rules, including the DPDP Act 2023 and IT Rules 2021
  • Runs on low-cost shared hosting, with no Node.js, Redis or background workers on the server
  • Ready to expand beyond India, with country-aware phone validation and currencies
Outcome

Where it landed

Clients find the right lawyer with a few filters, the firm stays in control of every enquiry and every listing, and it all runs on inexpensive hosting.
