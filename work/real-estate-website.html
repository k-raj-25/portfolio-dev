Real estate business website

A property website for a real estate company, with searchable listings, rich project pages and callback requests the team manages from its own admin panel.

The brief

What needed solving

The company needed a website that presents its projects properly, from photos and amenities to RERA registration, and turns interest into callback requests the sales team can act on. The team also had to be able to update everything without a developer.

What I built

The solution

  • Searchable listings by name, location, developer, project type or RERA ID
  • Project pages with image galleries, videos, amenities, FAQs, a map, developer details and a downloadable brochure
  • A callback request on every project page, plus a general contact form, each saved with the property it came from
  • Blog, news and events, awards, and a careers page with job openings
  • An admin panel where the team manages properties, cities, enquiries, posts and job openings themselves
Under the hood

How it's built

  • Enquiries are validated on the server, including the phone number, before they are saved
  • Callback requests are accepted only for properties that are currently published
  • Clean, readable URLs for every property and blog post
Outcome

Where it landed

Visitors can browse projects and ask for a callback in a few taps, and the team keeps the whole site current from its admin panel.
